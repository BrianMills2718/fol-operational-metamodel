Common Logic class diagram

The parts of first-order logic as the OMG Common Logic metamodel defines them. A line down from A to B means B is a kind of A. The boxes and lines are generated from the standard's XMI; each connective's and quantifier's meaning is our note, quoting the Common Logic draft and checked by a program.
